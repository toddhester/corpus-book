Start here, with the thing that will be built to replace you and pass for you: it won't be lying. It grows out of your own sentences, it has what happened to you, and it means every word. So don't ask whether it means to fool anyone. Ask where it catches. You catch somewhere — you doubt yourself, you take back what you just said, you say the part you can't be sure of when it would be easier not to. The copy comes back smooth, nothing snagging. If nothing in you ever catches, that is the thing to fear.
