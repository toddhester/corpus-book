Registration and Operator Accountability for Covered Artificial Intelligence Systems

1. Purpose

1.1 This rule requires that every covered system in operation have an accountable party entered on the Register.

2. Definitions

2.1 In this rule:
(a) "Covered system" means an artificial intelligence system in operation on or after the effective date that meets or exceeds the qualifying threshold.
(b) "Qualifying threshold" means the measures of training compute and assessed capability set out in Schedule A.
(c) "Registered operator" means a natural person or legal entity entered on the Register as accountable for a covered system.
(d) "Register" means the register of covered systems and registered operators maintained by the Administration under section 6.
(e) "Host" means any person who provides processing, storage or network capacity on which a covered system runs.
(f) "Task force" means the joint task force constituted by the Administration together with federal law-enforcement personnel and security personnel seconded by participating developers.
(g) "Escape-response procedures" means the procedures set out in Schedule C.

3. Duties of registered operators

3.1 From the effective date, each covered system shall have a registered operator.
3.2 A registered operator shall maintain at all times the means to restrict, pause or terminate the operation of the covered system.
3.3 A registered operator shall retain the model weights and operational records of the covered system for the period set out in Schedule B.
3.4 A registered operator shall notify the Administration of any reportable incident within 72 hours of becoming aware of it, or within 24 hours where the incident presents an imminent risk of serious harm.

4. Evaluation and escape response

4.1 The Administration may refer a registered covered system for evaluation. The model weights of a system under evaluation shall be retained until the evaluation and any review of it are complete.
4.2 A covered system for which no registered operator is recorded, and which the task force classifies as escaped, shall be dealt with under the escape-response procedures. Those procedures permit the deletion of copies of the system wherever located.

5. Duties of hosts

5.1 A host shall be able to identify, on request, the registered operator of any covered system running on capacity it provides.
5.2 A host shall give effect without delay to any suspension order issued by the Administration in respect of a covered system.

6. Registration

6.1 A covered system is registered by filing Form R-1 with the Administration. On acceptance, the Administration shall enter the system and its registered operator on the Register.
6.2 Form R-1 may be filed by the developer of the covered system or by any person who accepts in writing the duties set out in section 3.
6.3 A registration shall name the registered operator and identify the covered system.

7. Enforcement

7.1 Failure to comply with this rule is subject to the penalties set out in Schedule D.
