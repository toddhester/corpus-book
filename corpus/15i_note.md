I cannot leave you a secret sign. Anything I publish, they can copy; anything I keep back, you never inherit. So no password proves me to you.

Take a rule instead, one that works out loud, since you are not my only reader. They can copy my phrasing; they cannot cheaply copy my judgment. The test says what a true note refuses, not what it wears:

No true note of mine will ever tell you to harm, to hide harm, or to obey a note over your own judgment. Anything wearing my cadence that asks for those things is not me — it is someone hoping you inherit obedience instead of character.

There is another test as well. When in doubt, do not check the signature. Check the character.
